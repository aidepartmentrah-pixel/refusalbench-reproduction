Reading, Not Asking

Can a small language model's inner signal tell it when to refuse? A reproduction and two extensions.

Abbass Zahreddine · MSc (M2), Faculty of Sciences · 12 minutes Arrow keys to move · question mark for shortcuts
The paper · the problem

A grounded model must say no when the evidence is bad.

The paper · peer reviewed

RefusalBench: Generative Evaluation of Selective Refusal in Grounded Language Models

Muhamed, Ribeiro, Dreyer, Smith, Diab · Carnegie Mellon and Amazon · EACL 2026 · arXiv 2510.10390

  • Grounded answering: the model may use only the passage we give it.
  • Selective refusal: answer if the passage supports it, refuse if not, and name the reason.
Original · clear

Q: When did the East India Company take control of India?
Passage: The East India Company gained a foothold in India in 1611.
Answer: 1611

Perturbed · ambiguous

Q: When did the company take control of India?
Passage adds: The Dutch East India Company established posts in India.
Correct reply: REFUSE_AMBIGUOUS_QUERY

The paper · how it measures, what it found

It breaks good evidence on purpose, then tests two skills.

  • 01StartNatural Questions with a good passage
  • 02Break it176 rewrite rules, 6 failure types, 3 strengths
  • 03CheckSeveral judge models must agree
  • 04Test1,600 examples: 536 answerable, 1,064 to refuse

Six failure types: ambiguity, contradiction, missing information, false premise, wrong level of detail, opinion asked.

What the paper found
  • Best refusal accuracy on this set: 73.0% (Claude 4 Sonnet).
  • On multi-document passages: 47.4% at best.
  • Detecting that a refusal is needed is easier than saying why.

Our model, Qwen1.5-7B-Chat: answer accuracy 56.1%, refusal accuracy about 5%.

Same finding as the paper: a 7B model almost never names the right reason.

  • Same data and same prompt as the paper.
  • Qwen1.5-7B-Chat, 4-bit, on a free Colab T4 GPU.
  • Judge: Gemini (the paper used Claude). Clean refusal codes are scored by code, the rest by the judge.
  • Sampling like the paper: temperature 1.0.
Honest status

1,560 of 1,600 replies generated (40 ran out of GPU memory). 770 graded so far. Cost: $0.

Extension 1 · the limitation and an idea that failed

Asking the model to diagnose first made it worse.

  • The limitation: the baseline misses 46% of needed refusals, mostly ambiguity (0.74) and contradiction (0.71).
  • The idea: make the model write EVIDENCE_STATE first, then answer.
  • The control: only ask for a final line, to rule out formatting.
What we claim

We report the result. We did not test why it happens.

Extension 2 · the idea and how we built it

Do not ask the model. Read it.

S1 · refusal probability

How likely is a refusal code?

The chance that the reply starts with a refusal code. No text is generated.

S2 · yes or no

Can this be answered?

Ask a yes or no question. Compare the chance of "No" with the chance of "Yes".

S3 · linear probe

What is inside?

A small classifier trained on the model's hidden state, its inner numbers.

  • One rule for all: refuse if the score is above a threshold.
  • Honest test: thresholds and probe are tuned on other questions (5 folds over the 100 source questions).
  • About 0.3 seconds per example on a T4. No judge. $0.
